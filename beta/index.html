Settings

Declination: how far a compass is off from true north here. Filled in automatically from NOAA's magnetic model when your spot changes; typing a value turns that off. Brookfield is about −4.0° (west). Time zone: leave on your phone's zone unless your spot is in another one.

How accurate is it? Checked against NASA, the IMO and PyEphem.

Times are shown to the minute (seconds dropped). Sun, moon and sky math runs on your phone (NOAA and Meeus equations). Like any website, GitHub (which hosts the app) and OpenStreetMap (map images) see your IP address, and OpenStreetMap sees which map area you view. Nothing else leaves your phone. Once opened, it works with no signal.

Sun Field

Welcome to Sun Field. It works out sunrise, work light, shadows and the sky for wherever you are. Start with your location:

Your location stays on your phone. "Not now" shows Brookfield, IL; tap Locate at the top any time.

A one-minute walk through every screen.

  1. Times Today's light: work light, dawn and dusk, golden and blue hour, solar noon and the seasons. Switch between Extended (longer, wider) and Classic golden and blue hours.
  2. Pick a time Drag the light bar at the bottom to any time of day, pick a date, or tap Now to go back to live.
  3. Info pages Swipe the strip above the light bar: Sun, Twilight, Shadow, Moon, Moon phases, Milky Way and Sky today. On Shadow, tap ✎ to set an object's height.
  4. Dome The sky as a 3D bowl: today's sun path, the season paths, the moon, planets and stars. Drag to spin, pinch to zoom, Layers to turn things on or off, Live to make it follow your phone.
  5. Camera Point your phone at the sky to see the sun's path, the moon and planets over the camera. Use Calibrate and Align for the best aim.
  6. Map Sunrise and sunset lines, the sun's direction and the shadow from your spot. Tap the map to move your spot; Locate uses GPS.
  • Aim it right. Run Calibrate, then put the Camera crosshair on the sun (or the moon or a bright planet at night) and tap Align. One Align fixes both the Camera and the live Dome.
  • Keep metal away. Cars, railings, rebar and magnetic phone cases or MagSafe wallets pull the compass off by several degrees.
  • Works offline. Open it once with signal; after that it opens anywhere. Map areas you've looked at are saved too.
  • Make it an app. iPhone Safari: Share › Add to Home Screen. Android Chrome: ⋮ › Add to Home screen. Computer: browser menu › Install.
  • Brave users. Allow Motion sensors for this site (or turn Shields down), or Live and Camera won't move.
  • Plan shade and spacing. On the Shadow page, set an object's height (a fence, a tree, a panel) and drag the time to see how long its shadow gets.
  • Full-screen Dome or Map. Tap Hide sun info. On a computer, Aa Big text makes the sun info easy to read across the room.
  • Somewhere else? Tap the map or type coordinates in Settings, and pick that place's time zone if it's different from your phone's.
  • Find it useful? ⓘ › About › Support Sun Field.
  • Tell me what you think. ⓘ › About › Send feedback. Which phone and browser you used helps a lot.
  • Sky events. The Times tab lists the next 30 days: full moons, meteor peaks with the best hour to look, eclipses you can see from your spot, and the moon passing planets.
